# Code of conduct

We want GroundFlip's community to be rigorous, kind, and safe for contributors
of every background and experience level.

Be respectful. Critique claims and code with evidence, not people. Do not harass,
threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized language
or imagery. Assume good intent while accepting correction. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact or repository
moderation channel. Reports will be handled confidentially and conflicts of
interest will be disclosed. Consequences may range from a warning to permanent
exclusion, proportionate to severity and pattern.
